Finding a barber you trust is a bit like finding a good mechanic — once you've
got one, you stick with them. If you're new to Erin, Ontario, or just tired of
leaving the chair disappointed, here's how to tell a great barber from an
average one in 2026.

## 1. They start with a consultation

A good cut starts before the clippers come out. The barber should ask how you
wear your hair, what you liked or didn't like last time, and how much upkeep you
actually want. At Moe's Barbershop, every appointment — from a [Classic
Haircut](/services/mens-haircut) to the signature Moe's Haircut — opens with a
quick consultation so you leave with exactly the look you came for.

## 2. The fades are clean

A skin fade lives or dies by the blend. Look for seamless transitions with no
harsh lines, and crisp, even edges. If a shop can blend a [low, mid, high, burst
or drop fade](/services/skin-fade) cleanly down to the skin, they can handle just
about anything.

## 3. The reviews are real and recent

Ratings matter, but recency matters more. A barber with a steady stream of
recent 5-star reviews is consistently doing good work — not coasting on a few old
ones. Moe's Barbershop holds a 5.0 rating across 100+ Google reviews from clients
around Erin, Hillsburgh and the wider Wellington County area.
