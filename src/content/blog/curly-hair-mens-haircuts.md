Guys with curly hair often spend years fighting it — buzzing it short, brushing
it flat, or just putting up with a shape that puffs out by day three. But with
the right cut, curly hair is one of the best textures to work with.

Here's what we recommend at Moe's Barbershop.

## Why curly hair needs a different approach

Curls shrink. A curl that's three inches long when wet might sit at an inch and
a half when dry. That means a barber cutting curly hair has to account for
spring-back, cut with the curl pattern rather than against it, and avoid
over-thinning — which turns defined curls into frizz.

## 1. The curly fade

The most-requested curly cut in our chair. Clean [skin fade](/services/skin-fade)
on the sides with defined curls on top. The contrast makes the curls the star,
and the short sides stop the overall shape from getting wide and round.

**Best for:** tight to medium curls, guys who want a sharp, modern look.

## 2. The curly textured crop

A shorter version with a soft fringe falling forward. Easy to wear, works with
looser curls and waves, and grows out naturally.

**Best for:** wavy to loose curls, low-maintenance mornings.

## 3. The taper with length

Keep length all over with a [taper](/blog/taper-vs-fade-haircut) at the neckline and
sideburns. This lets curls form fully while keeping the edges clean.

**Best for:** guys growing their curls out who still want to look put together.

## 4. The curly burst fade

A [burst fade](/blog/skin-fade-guide-erin-ontario) curves around the ear and
leaves length at the back — a great match for curly mullets and modern mohawks.

## A simple curl care routine

- **Shampoo less.** Two or three times a week is plenty. Curly hair is naturally
  drier, and daily shampoo strips it.
- **Condition every time.** Conditioner is what keeps curls soft and defined.
- **Apply product to wet hair.** A curl cream or light leave-in conditioner,
  scrunched in while hair is damp, locks in definition.
- **Don't towel-rub.** Squeeze water out with a t-shirt or microfibre towel.
- **Hands off while it dries.** Touching curls as they dry causes frizz.

## Keep it shaped

Curly fades look best every **two to three weeks**. Longer curly styles can go
**five to six weeks** between visits — just enough to keep the shape balanced and
the edges clean.

## Bring your curls as they are

For the best result, come in with your hair styled the way you normally wear it
— not freshly brushed out or slicked down. It helps us see your natural pattern
and cut to it.
