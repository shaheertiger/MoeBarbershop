The right beard does more than cover your chin — it reshapes your whole face. A
well-chosen style can add length, carve out a jawline, or balance a wide
forehead. The wrong one can make your face look rounder, longer or heavier than
it really is.

Here's how we match beards to face shapes at Moe's Barbershop.

## Step one: find your face shape

Pull your hair back, look straight into a mirror and compare three widths —
forehead, cheekbones and jaw — against the length of your face.

| Face shape | What you'll notice |
| --- | --- |
| Round | Width and length are similar; soft jaw and full cheeks |
| Square | Width and length are similar; strong, angular jaw |
| Oval | Longer than wide; balanced forehead and jaw |
| Long / oblong | Noticeably longer than wide; long chin |
| Heart / triangle | Wider forehead, narrower jaw and pointed chin |

## Round face: add length, keep the sides tight

The goal is to make the face look longer and more defined.

- **Try:** a boxed beard, a short full beard with a squared chin, or a goatee
  extension.
- **Keep:** the sides short and faded into the hairline, with more length at the
  chin.
- **Avoid:** bushy sides — they widen the face.

## Square face: soften the angles

You've already got a strong jaw; the beard should complement it, not exaggerate
it.

- **Try:** a short, rounded full beard, a circle beard, or heavy stubble.
- **Keep:** a slightly rounded shape at the chin.
- **Avoid:** a sharp, blocky bottom edge, which makes the face look boxy.

## Oval face: you can pull off almost anything

Balanced proportions mean most styles work.

- **Try:** a classic full beard, a short boxed beard, or clean stubble.
- **Keep:** the shape balanced — not too long at the chin.

## Long face: add width, not length

Here you want to fill out the sides and keep the chin short.

- **Try:** a fuller beard on the cheeks, a chin-strap style with some width, or a
  mutton chop–inspired shape.
- **Keep:** the chin trimmed short and the cheek line a little lower.
- **Avoid:** long, pointed beards — they stretch the face further.

## Heart-shaped face: add weight at the jaw

A narrower jaw benefits from a beard that adds fullness lower down.

- **Try:** a full beard with some width at the jaw, or a "Garibaldi"-style
  rounded beard.
- **Keep:** the moustache moderate so it doesn't draw attention upward.
