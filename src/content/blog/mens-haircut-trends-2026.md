Every year brings a new wave of requests into the chair. Here's what guys are
asking for at Moe's Barbershop in Erin in 2026 — and, more importantly, who each
style actually suits.

## The textured crop

Still the most versatile cut going. Short and tidy on the sides with choppy,
textured length on top that you can style in seconds. It suits most face shapes
and hair types, and it grows out cleanly — ideal if you can't get to the shop
every two weeks.

## The low skin fade

Subtle, sharp and office-friendly. A [low skin fade](/services/skin-fade) keeps
most of your length while adding a clean, modern edge near the neckline. Great if
you want something fresh without going dramatic.

## Mid-length flow

Longer on top with natural movement, grown out over the ears. It's a bigger
commitment to grow and style, but it pays off for guys with thicker, wavier hair
who want a relaxed, modern look.

## The classic side part

Timeless and professional, the side part is back with cleaner fades on the side.
A [classic cut](/services/mens-haircut) like this never really goes out of
style — which is exactly why it keeps coming back.

## Haircut + beard, fully connected

The biggest "trend" of all isn't a cut — it's the finish. Pairing your haircut
with a [connected beard fade and lineup](/services/haircut-and-beard) ties the
whole look together and is the difference between a good cut and a sharp one.
