The buzz cut is the simplest haircut there is — and one of the hardest to hide
behind. With no length to style, it's all about the right guard, a clean shape
and sharp edges. Done well, it looks deliberate and confident. Done badly, it
just looks like you gave up.

Here's what we tell clients at Moe's Barbershop before they go short.

## Choosing your length

Buzz cuts are measured by clipper guard. (For the full breakdown, see our
[clipper guard numbers guide](/blog/clipper-guard-numbers-explained).)

| Guard | Length | Look |
| --- | --- | --- |
| #1 | ~3 mm | Very short; scalp clearly visible |
| #2 | ~6 mm | Short and clean; a little scalp shows |
| #3 | ~10 mm | The most popular all-round buzz |
| #4 | ~13 mm | Soft, fuller look; great for first-timers |

**First buzz?** Start at a #4 or #3. You can always go shorter next time.

## Buzz cut styles

### Induction cut

One short length all over, usually a #1 or #2. The most minimal option.

### Butch cut

Even length all over at a slightly longer guard, often a #3 or #4. Clean and
classic.

### Fade buzz

A buzz on top blended into a [skin fade](/services/skin-fade) on the sides. The
contrast adds shape and makes the cut look far more styled.

### Burr cut

Close to an induction cut but slightly longer, with a clean, squared hairline.

### Crew cut

Technically a step up from a buzz — a little more length at the front, tapering
toward the crown. A good option if you want the feel of a buzz with more shape.

## Does it suit your face shape?

- **Oval and square faces:** ideal for any buzz length.
- **Round faces:** go for a fade buzz — shorter sides add definition.
- **Long faces:** keep the top a touch longer (#3–#4) and avoid very high fades.
- **Thinning hair:** a buzz can be the sharpest option. Our [thinning hair
  guide](/blog/haircuts-for-thinning-hair) has more.

## What makes a buzz cut look professional

- **A sharp lineup.** A crisp hairline at the forehead and temples is the
  difference between a barbershop buzz and a home job.
- **A clean neckline.** Squared or rounded, it should be tidy and even.
- **A beard that connects.** If you have one, a [beard fade and
  lineup](/services/beard-trim-and-lineup) ties the whole look together.

## Looking after it

- **Wear sunscreen on your scalp** in summer — short hair offers little
  protection.
- **Moisturise in winter.** Short hair means a more exposed scalp. See our
  [fall and winter care tips](/blog/fall-winter-hair-scalp-care-ontario).
- **Book every two to three weeks** to keep the shape and edges crisp.
