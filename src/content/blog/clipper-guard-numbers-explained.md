"Number two on the sides, please." It's one of the most common requests we hear
at Moe's Barbershop — and one of the most misunderstood. Clipper guard numbers
are a handy shorthand, but only if you know what they actually leave behind.

Here's the cheat sheet.

## Clipper guard sizes at a glance

| Guard | Length (inches) | Length (mm) | What it looks like |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| #0 (no guard) | ~1/16" | ~1.5 mm | Very close; scalp clearly visible |
| #0.5 | 1/16" | 1.5 mm | Used to soften a fade near the skin |
| #1 | 1/8" | 3 mm | Short buzz; scalp shows through |
| #1.5 | 3/16" | 4.5 mm | Popular for blending fades |
| #2 | 1/4" | 6 mm | Short and neat, little scalp showing |
| #3 | 3/8" | 10 mm | Classic short sides |
| #4 | 1/2" | 13 mm | Short but full; good for a longer buzz |
| #5 | 5/8" | 16 mm | Soft, natural length |
| #6 | 3/4" | 19 mm | Longer sides, more coverage |
| #8 | 1" | 25 mm | The longest standard guard |

Guard lengths vary slightly between clipper brands, so treat these as close
approximations rather than exact measurements.

## Where a skin fade fits in

A [skin fade](/services/skin-fade) doesn't use one number — it uses several. We
start at the skin with a foil shaver or open blade, then work up through the
half sizes (#0.5, #1, #1.5, #2) to blend into the length on top. That gradual
step-up is what gives a fade its smooth, seamless look.

## Darker vs. lighter hair

The same guard can look very different depending on your hair. On dark, thick
hair, a #2 still looks fairly full. On fine or light-coloured hair, a #2 can
show quite a bit of scalp. If you're not sure, go one number longer — we can
always take more off, but we can't put it back.

## How to ask for what you want

- **Sides and top:** "#2 on the sides, scissor-cut on top, about two inches."
- **A fade:** "Mid skin fade, blended into a #3, leave the top long."
- **A taper:** "Taper the neckline and sideburns, #3 on the sides."
- **A buzz:** "#4 all over, square up the neckline."

Bring a photo if you have one. Numbers plus a picture is the fastest way for
any barber to get it exactly right.
