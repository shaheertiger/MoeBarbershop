Some of our favourite regulars at Moe's Barbershop have been getting their hair
cut the same way for decades — and they look great doing it. A good senior
haircut is about clean lines, an easy routine, and a comfortable, unhurried
visit.

Here's how we approach it.

## What changes with age

Hair often becomes finer, thinner or more wiry over time, and it may grow
differently around the crown and temples. Eyebrows and ear hair can become more
noticeable, and the scalp may be more sensitive. A good barber cuts with all of
that in mind.

## The cuts we recommend most

### The classic short back and sides

Tidy, timeless and easy to look after. Short and neatly tapered on the sides,
with a little length on top to comb into place.

### The side part

A gentleman's classic. A soft side part with tapered sides looks polished for
church, family events and everyday wear. It also works well with thinner hair.

### The crew cut

Short all over with a touch more length at the front. Practically no styling
needed — perfect for anyone who wants to wash and go.

### The silver statement

Grey and white hair can look incredibly distinguished when it's cut well. A
clean shape and sharp edges let the colour stand out for all the right reasons.

## The finishing details

The small things make a big difference:

- **Neckline and ears** — cleaned up so everything looks neat
- **Eyebrows** — trimmed of long strays (or tidied with a quick **threading**)
- **Beard or moustache** — shaped and lined up if you have one; ask about a
  [beard fade and lineup](/services/beard-trim-and-lineup)

## Comfort comes first

Our [Senior Haircut](/services/senior-haircut) is designed to be gentle and
unhurried. We take our time, keep it easy on the scalp, and are happy to take a
break if you need one. Just let us know if you have any mobility needs when you
book or arrive, and we'll make it work.
