Barbershops have their own rhythm, and if you're new to the chair — or new to
Ontario — the unwritten rules aren't always obvious. Here's a friendly guide to
barbershop etiquette from Moe's Barbershop in Erin.

## How much should you tip a barber in Ontario?

In Canada, the standard tip for a barber is **15% to 20%** of the service price.
For a great cut, a tricky style, or a busy day when we squeezed you in, many
clients go a little higher.

| Haircut & Beard | CAD$50 | $7.50 | $10 |
| Kids Haircut | CAD$25 | $3.75 | $5 |

Tipping is always appreciated, never required — and if something isn't right,
we'd much rather you tell us so we can fix it. For every service and price, see
our [men's haircut prices guide](/blog/mens-haircut-prices-erin-ontario).

## Arrive on time (or a few minutes early)

Appointments are scheduled back to back. Showing up five minutes early gives you
time to settle in and keeps the whole day running smoothly for everyone.

## Running late? Let us know

Life happens. If you're going to be late, call us at (519) 855-4455. A quick
heads-up lets us adjust — and sometimes still fit you in.

## Cancel instead of no-showing

If you can't make it, cancel or reschedule through your
[online booking](https://moes-barbershop.square.site/) or give us a call. That
slot can go to someone else who's waiting for a chair.

## Come with clean, dry hair (if you can)

You don't need to wash it right before, but clean hair without heavy product
makes the cut easier and more accurate — especially for fades and detailed work.
Curly-haired clients: come with your hair styled how you normally wear it.

## Know what you want — or be open

Bring a photo, or tell us your routine and let us recommend something. Both work
great. What's tough is "I don't know, just do something" followed by "that's not
what I wanted." Our guide on [what to tell your
barber](/blog/what-to-tell-your-barber) helps.

## Speak up during the cut

It's your hair. If something isn't right, say so — politely and early. A good
barber never takes it personally.

## Phones: quiet is golden

Scrolling is fine. Long speakerphone calls in the chair make it hard to hear
each other and harder to cut around your head. If you need to take a call, just
let us know and we'll pause.

## Walk-ins: be patient

Walk-ins are welcome when a chair is open, but booked clients come first. If you
want a guaranteed time, booking ahead is the way to go.

## Leave the review

If you loved your cut, a quick Google review means the world to a local shop.
It's how neighbours across Erin and Wellington County find us.
