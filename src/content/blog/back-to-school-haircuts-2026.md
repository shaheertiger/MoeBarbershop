The last two weeks of August are the busiest of the year at Moe's Barbershop.
Every family in Erin, Hillsburgh and the surrounding area wants their kids
looking sharp for the first day of school — and for school photos not long
after.

Here are the cuts we're seeing most for 2026, and a few tips for beating the
rush.

## Popular back-to-school cuts for 2026

### The textured crop

The number-one request for boys and teens. Short on the sides with a messy,
textured top that takes ten seconds to style — or none at all. It grows out
well, which makes it perfect for busy school months.

### The low or mid fade

A clean [fade](/services/skin-fade) on the sides is a teen favourite. A low or
mid fade keeps things sharp but not extreme, and it's school-dress-code-friendly
almost everywhere.

### The classic short cut

For younger kids or anyone who wants zero fuss: a short, neat cut with tapered
sides. Easy to wash, easy to dry, no product needed.

### The curly top

Lots of kids are embracing their natural texture with a short fade and curls on
top. See our [curly hair guide](/blog/curly-hair-mens-haircuts) for tips on
keeping curls defined.

### The design

Want to make the first day memorable? The **Kids Haircut + Design** adds a
custom shaved pattern — lines, zig-zags, or something unique — for just CAD$30.

## Tips for beating the back-to-school rush

1. **Book online early.** Appointments in the last week of August fill fast.
   [Book online](https://moes-barbershop.square.site/) to lock in a time.
2. **Go a week before school starts.** A cut about five to seven days before the
   first day has time to settle so it looks natural, not freshly-buzzed.
3. **Plan around school photos.** Picture day is usually a few weeks in. A quick
   tidy-up the week before keeps the look fresh.
4. **Come on a weekday morning.** If your kids aren't back yet, weekday mornings
   are typically quieter than evenings and weekends.

## Keep it easy for mornings

Pick a cut that matches your kid's routine. If they won't touch a comb, steer
toward a short crop or classic cut. Teens willing to use a little matte clay can
handle more length on top.
