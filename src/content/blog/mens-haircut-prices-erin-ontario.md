## What's included in a men's haircut

Every cut at Moe's starts the same way: a **quick consultation**. We ask how you
wear your hair, what you liked (or didn't) about your last cut, and how much
upkeep you actually want. Then comes the cut itself, the detail work — clean
necklines, sharp edges around the ears — and a finish styled the way you'll wear
it.

A few things worth knowing:

- **Fades are part of the cut.** A low, mid, high or [skin fade](/services/skin-fade)
  is done within the Classic Haircut, so skin fades start at $30.
- **The signature Moe's Haircut is the full experience.** It's a full hour: an
  unhurried consultation, a precision cut, detailed finishing and a hot-towel
  finish. Book it when you want a new style dialled in or just want to be looked
  after properly.
- **Beard work is its own service** unless you book the combo — which brings us
  to the easiest way to save.

## Why haircut prices vary from shop to shop

If you've compared barbershops around Wellington County, you've probably noticed
prices aren't all the same. The biggest factors are:

1. **Time in the chair.** A clean skin fade takes longer than a quick clipper
   cut, because the blend is done in stages.
2. **What's included.** A consultation, a lineup, a hot towel and styling all
   take time — some shops include them, some don't.
3. **Skill and consistency.** The difference between a good fade and a great one
   is years of practice. It's why reviews matter: Moe's holds a 5.0 rating across
   100+ Google reviews.

The cheapest cut isn't a deal if you have to get it fixed a week later. Pick the
barber whose regular work you'd happily wear every day.

## How much should you tip a barber in Canada?

Tipping is always optional, but for good service **15–20% is common in Canada**.
In practice that looks like:

- $30 Classic Haircut → about **$4.50–$6**
- $50 Haircut & Beard → about **$7.50–$10**
- $25 Kids or Senior Haircut → about **$3.75–$5**

We accept **cash, debit and credit cards**, so pay however suits you.
