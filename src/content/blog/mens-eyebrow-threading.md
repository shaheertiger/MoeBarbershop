A fresh haircut and a sharp beard lineup make a huge difference — but plenty of
guys don't realize how much their eyebrows frame the whole look. A quick
threading session tidies things up without making it look "done."

Here's everything you need to know about eyebrow threading at Moe's Barbershop.

## What is threading?

Threading is a hair-removal technique that uses a twisted cotton thread to lift
stray hairs out from the root. The barber rolls the thread along the skin,
catching rows of small hairs at once. It's fast, precise and works especially
well for shaping brows.

## Why guys choose threading

- **Precise.** Thread can grab even very fine, short hairs, so the shape comes out
  clean and even.
- **Natural results.** For men, the goal isn't a sculpted arch — it's removing the
  strays and the unibrow so your brows look tidy and natural.
- **Gentle on skin.** No chemicals and no hot wax, which makes it a good option
  for sensitive skin.
- **Fast.** Our threading service takes about five minutes.
- **Long-lasting.** Hair is removed from the root, so results typically last a
  few weeks.

## Threading vs. waxing vs. plucking

| | Threading | Waxing | Plucking |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Precision | High | Medium | High |
| Speed | Fast | Fast | Slow |
| Skin irritation | Low | Medium | Low |
| Good for fine hairs | Yes | Yes | Hard to grab |
| DIY friendly | No | Not really | Yes, but easy to overdo |

## What men usually ask for

- **Unibrow clean-up.** The most common request — clearing the space between the
  brows.
- **Stray removal.** Tidying the hairs above and below the brow line.
- **Light shaping.** A subtle, straight, masculine shape — never thin or overly
  arched.

Tell us "keep it natural" and we will.

## Does it hurt?

There's a quick pinch, similar to plucking, but because threading removes many
hairs at once, it's over in moments. Most guys find it easier than they
expected, and it gets more comfortable with regular visits.

## Aftercare

- Avoid touching the area for a few hours.
- Skip heavy creams or makeup on the area for the rest of the day.
- A little redness is normal and usually fades within an hour.
