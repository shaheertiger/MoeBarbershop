Thinning hair is incredibly common — and one of the things guys feel most
self-conscious about in the chair. The good news is that the right haircut can
make a big difference. The wrong one (usually: too long, trying to cover too
much) makes thinning more obvious, not less.

Here are the cuts we recommend most at Moe's Barbershop for thinning hair and
receding hairlines.

## The golden rule: shorter sides, controlled top

When the top is thinner than the sides, the contrast draws attention to it.
Taking the sides shorter — with a taper or a [skin fade](/services/skin-fade) —
balances everything out and makes the top look denser by comparison.

## 1. The textured crop

Our number-one recommendation. A short, choppy top pushed forward with a little
texture naturally covers a receding hairline without looking like you're hiding
anything. Pair it with a mid or low fade.

## 2. The short crew cut

Clean, classic and confident. A crew cut keeps the top short enough that
thinning areas don't stand out, with just enough length to show some shape at
the front.

## 3. The buzz cut

If you're thinning significantly, an even buzz — around a #2 to #4 — is often
the sharpest option. It removes the contrast completely and looks deliberate.
Add a clean lineup and a well-groomed beard and it's a strong look.

## 4. The short side part

For guys with a receding temple on one side, a short side part can be a smart
move. A hard or soft part on the deeper side frames the hairline naturally.
Keep the length on top modest so it doesn't look combed over.

## What to avoid

- **Growing it long to cover the area.** Long, fine hair separates and shows
  more scalp, not less.
- **Combovers.** They draw the eye exactly where you don't want it.
- **Heavy, shiny products.** Greasy pomades clump hair together. Use a matte
  clay or texture powder instead.

## Let the beard do some work

A well-shaped beard adds weight to the lower half of your face and balances out
a thinner top. A [beard fade and lineup](/services/beard-trim-and-lineup) — or a
[haircut and beard combo](/services/haircut-and-beard) — is one of the easiest
upgrades for guys with thinning hair.

## Keep it fresh

Short cuts for thinning hair look best when they're kept sharp. Most clients
come in every three to four weeks to keep the shape tight.
