For a lot of kids, the best part of coming to the barbershop isn't the haircut —
it's the design. A few clean lines or a custom pattern shaved into a fade makes
an ordinary cut feel special, and it's one of the most popular requests we get
for kids at Moe's Barbershop.

Here's what to know before you book.

## What is a hair design?

A hair design is a pattern cut into short hair — usually on the side or back of
the head — using a trimmer and a straight edge. It's typically added on top of a
fade or short cut, where the contrast between the hair and the skin makes the
lines stand out.

## Popular design ideas

### Single or double part line

The classic starter design. A clean shaved line along the part or above the ear.
Subtle, sharp and school-friendly.

### Lightning bolt

A favourite with younger kids. A zig-zag bolt on one side looks bold and is easy
to recognise.

### Curved swoosh

A sweeping curve that follows the shape of the head. Simple but stylish.

### Zig-zags and waves

Repeating lines around the side or back of the head. Great for kids who want
something more detailed.

### Stars and simple shapes

Small stars, arrows or geometric shapes. Bring a picture and we'll tell you
what will work at your child's hair length.

### Custom ideas

Initials, sports themes or something your child has designed themselves. Show
us a photo or sketch during the consultation and we'll talk through what's
possible.

## Tips for the best result

- **Shorter hair shows designs best.** Designs are usually cut into a fade or
  very short sides. On longer hair the lines can get hidden.
- **Thick, dark hair gives the most contrast.** Lighter or finer hair can still
  work — we'll just recommend bolder, simpler shapes.
- **Keep it simple the first time.** A single line is a great way to see how your
  child likes it before going bigger.
- **Bring a photo.** It's the fastest way to get the design your child is
  picturing.

## How long does it last?

Most designs look crisp for **one to two weeks**. As hair grows in, the lines
soften and fade into the cut. Many families book a quick refresh to keep it
sharp, especially before school photos or a special event.
