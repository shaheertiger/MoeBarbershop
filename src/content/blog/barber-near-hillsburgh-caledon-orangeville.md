Not everyone in our chair lives in Erin. A big part of our regulars come from
neighbouring towns — Hillsburgh, Caledon, Orangeville, Georgetown, Acton,
Rockwood, Guelph and further afield. When someone's driving past other shops to
get to us, we take that seriously.

Here's what they tell us makes the trip worthwhile, and how to plan your visit.

## Why clients make the drive

### Consistent, detailed cuts

The number-one reason: the cut is the same every time. Every appointment starts
with a consultation and finishes with clean edges, whether it's a [classic
haircut](/services/mens-haircut), a [skin fade](/services/skin-fade) or a
[beard fade and lineup](/services/beard-trim-and-lineup).

### A 5.0 rating, earned over time

Moe's Barbershop holds a 5.0 rating across 100+ Google reviews. A lot of those
reviews come from clients outside Erin who found us, tried us once, and kept
coming back.

### One shop for the whole family

Dads, sons, grandfathers — we handle [kids' cuts](/services/kids-haircut),
[senior cuts](/services/senior-haircut) and everything in between, so families
can book back-to-back and make one trip.

### Open seven days a week

We're open every day, including weekends, which makes it easier to fit a visit
around work and the commute.

## Tips for out-of-town clients

1. **Book ahead.** If you're driving in, [book online](https://moes-barbershop.square.site/)
   so your chair is ready when you arrive — no waiting around.
2. **Combine services.** A [haircut and beard combo](/services/haircut-and-beard)
   gets everything done in one 30-minute visit.
3. **Book your next one before you leave.** Lock in the same time on your usual
   rhythm so the trip becomes routine.
4. **Make a stop of it.** Erin's main street has cafés and shops worth a wander
   before or after your cut.
