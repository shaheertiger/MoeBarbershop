When you open our [booking page](https://moes-barbershop.square.site/), there
are two main haircut services for adults: the **Classic Haircut** and the
**Moe's Haircut**. Both get you a sharp result. The difference is time, detail
and what you're looking for.

Here's how to choose.

## At a glance

| | Classic Haircut | Moe's Haircut |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | CAD$30 | CAD$100 |
| Time | ~20 mins | ~1 hr |
| Consultation | Quick check-in | In-depth consultation |
| Best for | Regular maintenance, known styles | New looks, detailed cuts, special occasions |

## The Classic Haircut

The [Classic Haircut](/services/mens-haircut) is the cut most of our regulars
book on their usual two-to-four-week rhythm. It's built for timeless,
professional styles — tapers, fades, side parts, crops — done quickly and done
right.

**Book the Classic Haircut if:**

- You already know the cut you want
- You're coming in for regular upkeep of the same style
- You want a sharp result in about 20 minutes

## The Moe's Haircut

The Moe's Haircut is our signature service: a full hour in the chair,
starting with an in-depth consultation and finishing with a precision cut
designed around your face shape, hair type and the way you actually style your
hair.

**Book the Moe's Haircut if:**

- You want a **new look** and aren't sure exactly what it should be
- You're **growing out** a style and want a plan for getting there
- You have **tricky hair** — strong cowlicks, thinning areas, very curly or very
  thick hair — that benefits from extra time
- You have a **big event** coming up, like a wedding, job interview or photo
  shoot (see our [wedding haircut timeline](/blog/wedding-haircut-timeline-groom))
- You simply want an unhurried, detailed experience

## A smart way to use both

Plenty of clients book a Moe's Haircut to set a new style, then switch to the
Classic Haircut to maintain it. The detailed cut sets the shape; the regular
cuts keep it sharp.

## Don't forget the beard

If you have a beard, the [Haircut & Beard combo](/services/haircut-and-beard)
(CAD$50, ~30 mins) is the most efficient way to get both done and connected in a
single visit. You can also add a [Beard Fade & Lineup](/services/beard-trim-and-lineup)
or a five-minute threading touch-up to either haircut.
